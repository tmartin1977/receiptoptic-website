Security & Privacy

Your financial data is protected at every layer, both on your device and in transit.

Encrypted Local Storage

Your receipt databases, scanned images, and budget configurations are stored locally on your device. Local databases are fully encrypted and protected using SQLCipher, secured by your user password.

Encrypted Cloud Sync

If you choose to enable optional cloud synchronization, your database is securely synced between your devices via a private Supabase instance using industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption.

You Control Your Data

All local data resides on your device and is deleted if you uninstall the application. You can also delete your entire account and all associated cloud data instantly from the Settings screen.

Your Bank Login Stays With Plaid

When you link a bank account, your credentials are entered directly into Plaid's secure interface — never ReceiptOptic's app or servers. We only receive a secure token from Plaid to sync your transactions, never your bank username or password.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about how ReceiptOptic handles offline access, sync, receipt scanning, taxes, and your data.

Is there a good alternative to Copilot Money for offline expense tracking?

ReceiptOptic is built offline-first: you can scan and log receipts even without a connection, and anything captured while you're offline is safely queued locally and processed the moment you're back online. Your receipt database and scanned images stay encrypted on your device using SQLCipher, so your expense history is available and secure whether or not you have signal.

Does ReceiptOptic sync automatically like Monarch Money?

ReceiptOptic offers optional cloud synchronization that keeps your data updated across your devices automatically once enabled, using a private Supabase instance secured with SSL/TLS encryption. Sync is opt-in — your local encrypted database is always the source of truth on each device, and you can turn cloud sync on or off at any time from Settings.

How does ReceiptOptic scan and read my receipts?

Snap a photo of any receipt and ReceiptOptic's cloud AI automatically extracts the vendor, line items, and tax amounts. Saved vendor rules speed up categorization for merchants you've scanned before, so recurring purchases get sorted in seconds.

Can ReceiptOptic help with Schedule C and business tax tracking?

Yes. ReceiptOptic automatically maps itemized business expenses to IRS Schedule C tax lines and tracks your year-to-date deductions, giving you a real-time estimate of self-employment tax savings as you scan receipts throughout the year.

Is it safe to link my bank account through Plaid?

When you link a bank or investment account, your login credentials are entered directly into Plaid's secure interface — never into ReceiptOptic's app or servers. ReceiptOptic only receives a secure token from Plaid to sync balances and transactions, so your bank username and password are never seen or stored by us.

Where is my data stored, and is it encrypted?

Your receipt databases, scanned images, and budget configurations are stored locally on your device and fully encrypted with SQLCipher, protected by your user password. If you ever uninstall the app, that local data is removed with it, and you can permanently delete your account and any associated cloud data instantly from the Settings screen.

How to Install ReceiptOptic

Follow these simple steps to set up the desktop client on your Windows PC:

1

Download the ZIP

Wait for the download to complete saving `ReceiptOpticDesktop.zip` to your computer.

2

Extract the Files

Right-click the ZIP file and select "Extract All...". Do not run it from inside the compressed folder.

3

Launch the App

Open the extracted folder and double-click "ReceiptOptic.exe" to start.

4

Security Notice

If Windows SmartScreen shows a warning, click "More info" and then select "Run anyway" to run the app.

📲

Install ReceiptOptic

Add this app to your home screen for quick offline access.
